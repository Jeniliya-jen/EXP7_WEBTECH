TRAVEL WITH CONFIDENCE
GOOD TO KNOW

Little details. Better journeys.

Practical reminders to make your time at the station and onboard more comfortable.

01

Before leaving home

Recheck the journey date, station name and departure time. Keep your ticket reference and a valid photo ID easy to reach.

  • Charge your phone
  • Keep a power bank ready
  • Check your route to the station
02

At the station

Read the electronic display boards and listen for announcements. Confirm the platform using official station information.

  • Allow time for security and walking
  • Use marked foot overbridges
  • Ask authorised railway staff for help
03

On the train

Keep your belongings in sight and be considerate of fellow passengers, especially during night journeys.

  • Keep aisles and doors clear
  • Use headphones for audio
  • Dispose of waste responsibly
PLAN AHEAD

Travel seasons at a glance.

Demand can rise around holidays, festivals and school breaks. Start comparing options early and keep your plans flexible.

JAN – MARNew-year escapes

Consider long weekends and pleasant-weather city breaks. Check local event dates before finalising stays.

APR – JUNSummer holidays

Family travel can increase during school breaks. Compare dates early and plan extra station time.

JUL – SEPMonsoon journeys

Allow extra flexibility for road transfers and check weather and service advisories before departure.

OCT – DECFestival season

Popular dates may fill quickly around festive periods. Keep alternate dates and routes in mind.

TRAVEL COMFORT

Make the ride easier

Wear comfortable layers, keep water accessible where permitted, carry essential medication and store valuables securely. Be considerate of shared space and fellow passengers.

FLEXIBLE PLANNING

Have a back-up plan

Save your booking details, note an alternate local transport option and keep important contact information available offline in case mobile connectivity is limited.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Enter the numberUse the 10-digit PNR shown on your sample ticket.

STATUS KEY

What the status means

CNF

Confirmed — a coach and berth are assigned in the demo record.

WL

Waitlist — confirmation is still pending in the sample record.

CAN

Cancelled — the reservation is not active.

IMPORTANT NOTE

Demo enquiry, not live railway data

This academic portal does not connect to the official railway reservation system. It can show details only for bookings created and saved inside this browser.

Help & Information

Some instructions to use the RailDesk portal.

How to search for trains?

  1. Enter your departure station.
  2. Enter your destination station.
  3. Select your journey date.
  4. Choose your preferred travel class.
  5. Click Search Trains.
  6. Choose a train and enter passenger details.

How to save a booking?

  1. Search for a train.
  2. Click the Book button.
  3. Enter the passenger name and age.
  4. Confirm your reservation.
  5. View the saved ticket under My Bookings.

How do I check a PNR?

  1. Open PNR Enquiry from the top navigation.
  2. Enter the 10-digit number from a saved RailDesk ticket.
  3. Select Check PNR to view the locally stored demo status.

Where can I find travel planning tools?

Open Smart Tools for the class advisor, leave-home planner, budget calculator, group fare splitter, travel buddy suggestions, station guide, packing checklist, arrival alarm, journey ratings, swap board, complaint desk and eco comparison.

Why is my booking list empty?

Bookings are stored in your browser. If you have not completed a sample reservation on this device and browser, the list will show an empty state. Creating a booking from Train Search will add it here.

Can I use RailDesk for a real train reservation?

No. RailDesk is an academic demonstration project. Train availability, fares, PNR status, complaint tracking and cancellation protection are simulated and do not replace official railway services.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

A quick guide to using the RailDesk demonstration portal.

Are the train schedules and fares live?

No. This is an academic demonstration using sample train, fare and availability data. It is not connected to IRCTC or a live railway reservation system.

How do I search for a train?

Enter the source and destination stations, choose your journey date and travel class, then select Search Trains. Matching sample services appear in the results table.

Where can I find a ticket after booking?

Open My Bookings to view saved demonstration bookings. From the ticket confirmation, you can also print the ticket or use Share my journey where supported by your browser.

What can I do in Smart Travel Tools?

You can compare a suggested class, calculate when to leave for the station, estimate a trip budget, split group costs, view station tips and prepare a packing checklist.

Can I check a real PNR number here?

The PNR Enquiry page demonstrates the interface with sample records only. For actual booking status, use the official railway service associated with your ticket.

What should I do if my plans change?

Use the sample cancellation and complaint features to explore the portal workflow. For a real ticket, follow the cancellation and support instructions of the official booking provider.
